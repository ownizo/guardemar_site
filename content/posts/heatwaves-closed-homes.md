A closed house in a hot spell is still a closed house: little air movement, rooms that heat up, and systems that may or may not have been left running. The Algarve’s reputation for sunshine can make that feel harmless. Heat does not invent new defects on its own, but it can make odours, dried traps, stressed gardens and struggling equipment more obvious.

The response should stay within what can be seen, smelled and photographed. It should not become a new electrical or air-conditioning design.

## Air, odour and the closed-room problem

Bathrooms, kitchens and rooms with limited airflow can smell stale faster in heat. Drains that have dried may add to that. None of these observations diagnoses a building defect by itself. They are reasons to ventilate according to the owner’s instruction, to note the change, and to call a plumber if there is a sewage smell that does not belong.

Humidity and heat can coexist. Coastal air is still moist. Surface mould indicators remain a visual finding, not a medical one. See [humidity and mould](/blog/humidity-mould-algarve-holiday-homes/).

Leaving windows open “to let the house breathe” is not a default summer policy. It is a security and weather decision, and it should be written down.

## Air conditioning is not a substitute for a visit

Some owners leave a unit on a dry or low setting. That may help some interiors and may be irrelevant in others. Settings should follow the equipment guidance and a qualified technician’s advice where the system is unclear. A visual inspection can confirm that a unit is running if that check was agreed. It cannot certify the system, recharge gas or interpret unusual noises as a diagnosis.

Power used by air conditioning is part of the wider on-or-off conversation. See [electricity and water in an empty home](/blog/electricity-water-empty-holiday-home/).

## Gardens and pools take the heat first

Irrigation faults show sooner in a heatwave. A dry zone that would have been a mild concern in March can brown a bed in July. Pools lose water to evaporation *and* to leaks; comparison photographs help a pool company decide which they are looking at. See [irrigation during a long absence](/blog/irrigation-gardens-long-absence-algarve/) and [leaving a pool while you are away](/blog/leaving-pool-while-away-algarve/).

Outdoor furniture, covers and shade structures should be left as instructed so wind and heat are not improvising a new layout.

## What to agree before the hot months

Write whether anyone may open shutters for a visit and close them afterwards, whether air conditioning should be confirmed as running, and whether the gardener’s timer is expected to stay active. A heatwave is a poor moment to start that conversation from another country.

Inspection frequency may need to be higher when outdoor systems are working hardest. That is a property decision, not a slogan. [How often to inspect](/blog/how-often-inspect-empty-property-portugal/) remains the framework.

If your home will be closed through high summer, [request a property assessment](/contact/) or compare [plans](/plans/). Guardemar can observe and report; specialists remain responsible for equipment.
