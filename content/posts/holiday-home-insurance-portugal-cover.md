Most owners take out home insurance when they buy, often through the bank or the agent, and rarely read the policy again. For a second home that stays empty for months, that can be a mistake. Policies differ, and many contain conditions that matter specifically when nobody lives in the property.

This is a general overview. Only your policy wording, and a qualified insurance professional, can tell you what your own cover includes.

## The usual structure

Home insurance in Portugal is commonly sold as a multi-risk policy (seguro multirriscos habitação). It typically combines:

- **Buildings cover**: the structure, walls, roof, fixed installations and, depending on the policy, pools, walls and outbuildings.
- **Contents cover**: furniture, appliances and personal belongings.
- **Liability cover**: damage you, or your property, cause to others, such as water leaking into the flat below.

Additional cover, such as for accidental damage, legal protection or assistance services, is often optional.

## Risks commonly included

Policies commonly include fire, storms, water damage, theft and certain electrical risks. The details matter: what counts as a storm, whether gradual leaks are covered, and what conditions apply to theft.

## Points that matter for a second home

- **Unoccupancy.** Many policies have conditions or reduced cover when a property is unoccupied for a specified period. Read [unoccupied property clauses](/blog/unoccupied-property-clauses-documented-inspections/).
- **Gradual damage.** Slow leaks, damp and mould are often treated differently from sudden events.
- **Security conditions.** Some policies ask for particular locks, alarms or for the property to be secured in a certain way.
- **Valuables.** Jewellery, watches, art and cash may be limited or excluded, particularly in an unoccupied home.
- **Outdoor items.** Garden furniture, pool equipment and items left outside may not be covered for every risk.
- **Rental use.** If the property is let, even occasionally, the insurer may need to know.
- **Sums insured.** If the rebuild value or contents are under-insured, a claim may be reduced.
