Arriving at Faro airport, collecting the keys and finding that the car at the villa will not start is a familiar story. A flat battery, a soft tyre or a car covered in dust and bird droppings is rarely serious, but it is an unwelcome start to a stay.

Some of this can be prevented with simple checks. Some of it needs a mechanic. The distinction matters.

## What a visual check can cover

A visual check of a parked car can record:

- whether the car is where it was left and appears undamaged
- visible tyre condition, such as an obviously flat tyre
- whether doors, windows and the boot are closed and locked
- signs of fluid leaking beneath the car
- signs of animals, nests or insects around the wheels or under the bonnet area
- the condition of a car cover, if one is used
- dust, sap or salt build-up

Photographs taken in the same positions each visit make changes easy to spot.

## What may be agreed separately

Some owners ask for basic measures to help keep the car ready, such as connecting a battery maintainer supplied by the owner, or starting the engine for a short period where that is safe and legally permitted. These need specific written instructions, the owner's insurance position, and agreement that such measures are appropriate for the car.

Moving the car, driving it, taking it for inspection or to a garage are separate matters. They need express approval and the right insurance cover.

## What needs a mechanic

- battery replacement or diagnosis
- brake, tyre or suspension problems
- warning lights on the dashboard
- preparing a car for a long period of storage
- roadworthiness inspections

Ask your local garage what preparation it recommends before you leave, and keep its contact details in your property file.

## Practical tips before you leave

- leave the car with a full tank and correct tyre pressures
- clean the interior and leave nothing that attracts insects
- consider a battery maintainer, or ask your mechanic about disconnecting the battery
- leave the spare key, documents and remote in a secure place agreed in writing
- check insurance, annual vehicle tax and inspection dates. See [leaving a car in Portugal while you are abroad](/blog/leaving-car-portugal-while-abroad/)
