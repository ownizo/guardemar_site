A well-kept garden is one of the first things visitors notice and one of the first things to decline when nobody is watching. In the Algarve, a garden can go from tidy to tired in a single summer if irrigation fails or visits become irregular.

Owners usually choose between an independent gardener and a garden maintenance company. Both can work well. The difference lies in continuity, cover and communication.

## An independent gardener

Many Algarve homes have been looked after by the same local gardener for years. Their advantages are real: they know the plants, the irrigation and the quirks of the plot. They are often flexible and know the neighbours.

The risks tend to appear when that person is ill, on holiday or busy elsewhere. There may be no written schedule, no report and no clear way to know whether a visit happened.

## A garden maintenance company

A company usually offers more people, more equipment and cover when one person is unavailable. Some send reports or invoices that show visit dates. The trade-off can be less personal knowledge of the property, and different staff from one visit to the next.

## What to agree, whoever you choose

Write down a simple scope:

- visit frequency in summer and in winter
- what is included: mowing, pruning, weeding, hedge cutting, green waste removal
- who checks and adjusts the irrigation, and how often
- whether pool-area planting is the gardener's responsibility
- who keeps keys or gate codes
- how the gardener reports a problem, such as a broken pipe or a dying tree

For irrigation in particular, see [irrigation and gardens during a long absence](/blog/irrigation-gardens-long-absence-algarve/) and the current [Algarve garden watering rules](/blog/algarve-garden-watering-rules/).

## Land beyond the garden

Rural and semi-rural plots may have land-clearing obligations that go beyond ordinary gardening. These are legal duties with annual deadlines, not a question of appearance. Read [mandatory land clearing in Portugal](/blog/mandatory-land-clearing-portugal-foreign-owners/).
