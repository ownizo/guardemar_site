An empty villa is not static. Six months can include temperature changes, Atlantic weather, irrigation cycles, power interruptions and long periods without water moving through taps and drains.

## Moisture has time to develop

Closed rooms may hold humid air. Condensation can form around cooler surfaces and limited ventilation can allow musty odours or mould indicators to develop. The right response depends on the building, season and owner instructions; simply leaving every window open is neither secure nor appropriate.

## Small water issues become larger

A slow connection beneath a sink may remain invisible until cabinetry is affected. A toilet cistern can run, a seal can fail or a drain can dry and produce odour. Scheduled visual checks shorten the time between a problem appearing and an owner knowing about it.

## Exterior systems continue working

Irrigation can fail in either direction: plants may receive no water, or a damaged line may waste it. Pool water level and appearance can change even with a maintenance company. Gates, shutters and outdoor furniture remain exposed to wind.

## The value is in comparison

A single visit provides a snapshot. Repeated [property inspections](/inspection-checklist/) create context. The person checking the home can compare a new ceiling mark, changed pool level or dry garden area with the previous report.

For owners away for long periods, a [weekly, fortnightly or monthly plan](/plans/) creates a documented routine and one route for coordinating action.
