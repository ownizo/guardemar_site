Renovating a villa in the Western Algarve while living in another country is common, and often stressful. Progress photographs arrive from the builder, payments fall due on milestones, and the owner has to judge from a distance whether what is reported is what is happening on site.

## Start with the right professionals

Building work in Portugal involves licensed builders, and often an architect or engineer responsible for the project. Licensing, design and technical supervision are their responsibility, and they should be appointed before work starts. Nothing in this article replaces them.

What owners often lack is something simpler: an independent, regular look at the site by someone who works for them, not for the builder.

## Agree the record before the work starts

A few things make a remote project easier to follow:

- **A written programme.** Milestones with dates, so a visit can be compared against what should have happened.
- **A single channel.** Decide who reports to you, and in what form. Scattered messages from several people are hard to follow and harder to rely on later.
- **Payment tied to evidence.** Where a payment depends on a stage being complete, ask how completion will be shown.
- **Protection of what stays.** Floors, joinery, furniture and planting that are not part of the works should be protected, and their condition recorded before work begins.

## What independent visits add

On site visits we photograph the same areas each time, note who was working and what has visibly changed, and record anything that looks inconsistent with the programme or with your instructions — an unprotected floor, standing water, a window left open, materials stored where they should not be. You receive the report the same day.

We do not supervise the works technically, approve stages, certify quality or instruct the builder on your behalf. Those are matters for your architect, engineer or builder. Our role is to give you an independent, dated account of what was visible on the day.

## When the builders leave

The weeks after completion are when snags appear: a leak at a new shower, irrigation reconnected the wrong way, a door that no longer closes in damp weather.
