A second home in Portugal is often insured under a policy written for a pattern of use that the insurer has defined, not for the pattern you assume. Some products treat a holiday home differently from a main residence. Some care about how long the building is unoccupied. The only accurate source for *your* position is the policy, the insurer or an insurance adviser.

Guardemar does not sell insurance, interpret cover or predict a claim. The list below is what owners often need to ask. It is not a set of answers.

## Occupancy and absence

- How does this policy define an unoccupied or vacant property?
- After how many days without someone living there do different conditions apply?
- Do I need to tell you about a planned absence, and in what form?
- Is a holiday home treated differently from a let property or a main residence?

There is no responsible one-line definition that covers every Portuguese or international policy. Read the wording.

## Visits, water and security

- Are regular visits required, and what should a visit include?
- Do you expect water to be shut off, heating or air conditioning in a particular state, or alarms to be set?
- What records should be kept of those visits?
- Do locks, shutters or alarm monitoring have to meet a stated standard?

A documented [home watch](/home-watch-algarve/) visit can show that someone attended and what they saw. It cannot amend the contract or guarantee how a future claim will be assessed. See [unoccupied property clauses](/blog/unoccupied-property-clauses-documented-inspections/).

The water and electricity choice should be aligned with whatever the policy actually says, as well as with the building. [Should you leave electricity and water on](/blog/electricity-water-empty-holiday-home/) is the practical side of that conversation.

## Claims, notification and other people

- If damage is found, how quickly must you be told, and by whom?
- What should be preserved as evidence?
- Does cover change if family, guests or a house-sitter occupy the home?
- Does a contractor or keyholder attending affect anything I should know about?

When a concern is found, follow the policy’s notification steps as well as calling a plumber. A property-care company can document and coordinate. It cannot notify the insurer in your place unless you have arranged that separately.

## Keep the answers with the property

Write down what you were told, on which date, and from which document or person. Store it with inspection reports and invoices. [A simple evidence file](/blog/evidence-file-vacant-algarve-home/) is the practical home for that paper.

If the answers point to a need for regular documented visits, compare [plans](/plans/) or [request a property assessment](/contact/). Confirm the insurance requirements with the insurer or adviser, not with Guardemar.
