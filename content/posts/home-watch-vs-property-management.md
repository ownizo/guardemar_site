The terms *home watch* and *property management* are often used interchangeably, but they can describe very different priorities.

## Rental management follows the guest journey

Holiday rental management usually includes listing a property, setting rates, handling bookings, communicating with guests, organising changeovers and responding during stays. Its commercial focus is occupancy and guest experience.

## Home watch follows the property

Home watch is organised around the condition of an unoccupied or intermittently occupied home. A scheduled visit looks for visible signs of leaks, moisture, access problems, storm damage, electrical interruption and other changes. The owner then receives a record of what was found.

This is particularly useful when a property is primarily for private use, sits empty for weeks or months, or already has separate gardening, pool and cleaning providers.
