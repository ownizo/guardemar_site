Alvor combines modern apartments, resort properties and villas close to the estuary and the coast. Many of those homes are occupied only during part of the year. Summer can be busy. The months either side often are not.

For an overseas owner, the practical question is what happens to the property in the quiet period: humidity, marine air, balconies, shutters, mail, and the boundary between a condominium’s remit and the inside of the home.

## Apartments, resort properties and villas

The local mix is wide. An apartment in a shared development, a townhouse and a villa with a pool do not fail in the same way, and they should not be inspected as if they did.

In a shared building, access, waste, exterior cleaning and some building systems may sit with a condominium or resort operator. That does not inspect your kitchen cupboards, bathroom ceiling, shutters from the inside or the accessible plumbing under a sink. Exterior responsibilities should be clear so that a home-watch visit does not duplicate, or assume, work that belongs to someone else.

Villas add gates, gardens, irrigation and often a pool. Existing gardeners and pool companies may already visit. Their remit is specific. An independent visual check still connects the interior, access points and obvious exterior changes.

## Marine air, balconies and shutters

Alvor’s position near the estuary and coast makes marine air, humidity, balconies and shutters relevant across many property types. Salt and moisture can show first on external metalwork, window fittings and shutter mechanisms. A visual inspection can record obvious deterioration or a shutter that no longer sits as it did. It cannot restore the mechanism or assess concealed corrosion.

Balconies and terraces deserve a consistent photograph. Drainage outlets, outdoor furniture and any signs of water at thresholds are useful comparison points from one visit to the next. After unsettled weather, those same points are where change is most likely to be visible from safe ground.

Closed interiors still need attention. Bathrooms, wardrobes and rooms with limited air movement can develop musty odours or surface mould indicators during a long absence. The pattern is described in [humidity and mould in Algarve holiday homes](/blog/humidity-mould-algarve-holiday-homes/).

## Seasonal occupancy, not a constant presence

Seasonal ownership in Alvor often creates long quiet periods outside summer and shorter gaps between winter visits. That is a different rhythm from a home used every month. It makes a baseline photograph set more valuable, because the property may look very different in August and in November.

A single visit is a snapshot. Repeated visits, using the same [inspection checklist](/inspection-checklist/), show whether a stain is stable, an irrigation problem is recurring or a shutter has moved. Frequency should follow the home and season, not a slogan. See [how often to inspect an empty property](/blog/how-often-inspect-empty-property-portugal/).

Water remains the issue that grows with time. A slow leak in a vacant apartment can affect cabinetry, flooring or a neighbouring unit before anyone is in residence. The guide to [small water leaks in vacant homes](/blog/small-water-leaks-vacant-homes/) explains why early observation matters, without treating a visual check as a plumbing test.

## Access in shared and private settings

Resort and condominium access can be slower than a private gate. Instructions should name the people who may enter, how keys or fobs are held, and what to do if the building’s own rules change. [Key holding](/key-holding-algarve/) is there to keep that process controlled.

When a plumber, electrician or other provider is needed, [contractor access](/contractor-access-algarve/) records attendance and the visible condition afterwards. Guardemar coordinates; the specialist remains responsible for diagnosis and workmanship.

[Arrival preparation](/arrival-preparation-algarve/) is particularly useful after a long summer-to-winter gap, when the owner is travelling with family or guests and would rather learn of a concern before the flight.

## A local Alvor routine

Document how water, electricity, hot water, appliances and ventilation should be left. Keep condominium, resort, pool and garden contacts in the property file. Inspect on a schedule that matches vacancy, and keep photographs that an owner abroad can actually use.
