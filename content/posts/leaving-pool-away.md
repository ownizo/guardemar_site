A swimming pool is one of the systems that does not pause when the owner flies home. Water level, colour, debris and equipment can change between specialist visits. They can also change *despite* specialist visits, which is why an independent look at the pool still has a point.

It is a visual look. It is not water chemistry, leak detection, or a judgement on the pump.

## Keep the specialist in the plan

If you have a pool company, keep them. Dosing, filtration schedules, cover use and equipment servicing belong with that provider. A property-care visit that notes “the water looks green” or “the level is lower than last time” is useful because it is dated and photographed. It does not replace the person who tests the water.

Write down what the pool company will do while you are away, how they obtain access, and whether Guardemar should be told when they have attended. [Working with existing providers](/blog/working-with-existing-pool-garden-cleaners/) covers that coordination. [Contractor access](/contractor-access-algarve/) is the process if they need a keyholder.

If you pause pool maintenance for the winter, that pause should be a decision, not an accident. Someone still needs to look at the water, the cover and the immediate surroundings.

## What a visual inspection can record

From the pool surround, without entering the water or dismantling equipment, a visit can usually observe:

- whether the level looks similar to the last photograph
- whether the water is unusually cloudy, green or full of debris
- whether a cover is in place as instructed
- whether adjacent paving, drainage or irrigation is washing into the pool
- whether obvious equipment housings, lids or pipes look disturbed

Those observations become more useful when they are compared. A slightly low level after a hot week is not the same as a sudden drop. A photograph with a date is the difference.

Do not treat a visual check as proof that the shell is sound, the liner is intact or the pump is healthy. Those questions go to the pool professional.

## Pools sit next to other vacancy risks

Irrigation blowing into the pool, a cover blown off in wind, and outdoor furniture in the water are common after unsettled weather. They belong in the same visit as shutters and drainage, not in a separate “pool-only” story. See [storm property checks](/storm-property-checks-algarve/) and [irrigation during a long absence](/blog/irrigation-gardens-long-absence-algarve/).

Saharan dust can also settle on the surface and the surround. That is an appearance issue for the specialist to treat, not a reason to empty the pool on a guess. See [Saharan dust and calima](/blog/saharan-dust-calima-algarve-homes/).

## Access and safety

Pool areas should be left as the owner instructs: gates, covers, chemicals stored as the pool company requires. A home-watch visit should not handle chemicals or climb on fragile covers. If something looks unsafe, the report should say so and the specialist should be called.

Frequency follows the property. A villa whose pool is the main outdoor system may justify more regular oversight than an apartment with no water feature. Compare [plans](/plans/) after an assessment.

If your Algarve home has a pool that will sit unused, [request a property assessment](/contact/). Guardemar can observe and coordinate; the pool company remains responsible for the water.
