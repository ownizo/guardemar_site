When a house is used only part of the year, memory is a poor filing system. Instructions live in one inbox, a plumber’s invoice in another, and last winter’s leak exists only as a recollection. A simple evidence file is the opposite of that: a dated set of documents that shows what was supposed to happen, what was observed, and what was done.

It is not a claim file until a claim exists. It is not legal advice. It is housekeeping for a remote owner.

## What belongs in the file

Keep, in one place you can actually find:

- the current owner instructions (water, electricity, alarms, shutters, who may enter)
- key and access inventory, coded rather than addressed
- inspection reports and their photographs
- contractor attendance notes and invoices
- condominium or specialist contacts
- any written answers from an insurer or adviser about vacancy, visits or notification — stored as correspondence, not as Guardemar’s interpretation

[What a photo report should contain](/blog/what-photo-report-should-contain/) describes the visit side. [How to brief a property-care company](/blog/how-to-brief-property-care-company-algarve/) describes the instruction side. [Questions to ask your insurer](/blog/questions-to-ask-insurer-second-home-portugal/) is the insurance conversation to have elsewhere.

## Keep it factual

Write what was seen, when, and by whom. Separate observation from someone else’s diagnosis. “Moisture visible beneath the kitchen sink on 12 November” is a fact. “The pipework is defective” is a plumber’s job to say.

That distinction is what makes a chronology usable later — for a contractor, a buyer, or, if it comes to it, an insurer. [Unoccupied property clauses](/blog/unoccupied-property-clauses-documented-inspections/) explains the limit: records can support an account of oversight. They cannot promise cover.

## Photographs need a home as well as a caption

A report that says “see WhatsApp” will not survive a change of phone. Store the images with the visit date and the room. The same viewpoints each time are more valuable than a large unsorted album.

If a follow-up visit happens after a repair, keep that report next to the original. The story is then complete.

## Share only with people who need it

The file will contain access information, personal contact details and pictures of your home. Send it to the keyholder, the owner, and a contractor who has a reason to see a particular visit. Do not attach the full pack to every casual message.

When ownership or the local team changes, treat the file as part of the [property handover](/property-handover-algarve/).

## Start it at closing, not after a leak

The cheapest moment to create the file is when the house is in known condition — typically when you leave after summer. [Closing the house after summer](/blog/closing-algarve-home-after-summer/) is that moment. Every later visit then has something to compare.
