The best time to discover a problem is not late in the evening after travelling. A pre-arrival visit gives an owner an informed view of the home and creates time for practical follow-up.

## Inspect early enough

Arrange the visit several days before arrival where possible. That allows time to coordinate cleaning, pool or garden providers and technical trades if a visible problem is found.

## Check the basics

Confirm the property is accessible and visually secure. Follow agreed instructions for water and electricity. Check hot-water and air-conditioning status where authorised, look through kitchens and bathrooms, and note any odour, leak or power concern.

## Review outside areas

For villas, observe gate access, terraces, visible storm damage, pool appearance and garden condition. This remains a visual check; pool chemistry, electrical diagnosis and other technical work stay with specialists.

## Coordinate, do not improvise

Owner instructions should specify what can be switched on, what spending is authorised and which providers to contact. That prevents well-intentioned changes to systems the property owner wanted left untouched.
