Apartments in Lagos, Portimão, Alvor and Praia da Luz often come with a parking space in an underground garage and a small storage room (arrecadação). Owners rarely think about them. Yet these spaces are often where problems start: damp, flooding, theft and disputes with the condominium.

## Humidity and flooding

Underground garages are cool, poorly ventilated and sometimes below the level of the street. In heavy rain, water can enter through ramps, drains or walls. Storage rooms without ventilation collect damp, and anything stored in cardboard boxes, fabric or wood can develop mould.

Practical steps:

- store items on shelves, not on the floor
- use sealed plastic boxes instead of cardboard
- avoid storing documents, mattresses, clothing or anything valuable
- leave a gap between stored items and walls
- consider moisture absorbers, replaced regularly

See [humidity and mould in Algarve holiday homes](/blog/humidity-mould-algarve-holiday-homes/).

## Security

Shared garages have many users and frequent access. Bicycles, surfboards, tools and golf bags are common targets. Keep storage rooms locked with a good-quality padlock, record what is inside, and photograph valuable items. Do not leave remote controls or keys for the apartment in the car or storeroom.

## Condominium rules

Garages and storage areas are usually governed by the condominium regulations. They may cover long-term parking, storage of fuel, gas bottles or flammable materials, charging electric vehicles, and the use of common areas. If you plan to install an electric-vehicle charger, check the condominium procedures first.

The condominium administrator is also the right contact for problems in common areas, such as a flooding ramp or a broken garage door.

## Garage doors and remotes

Garage doors and gates often stop working after power cuts or storms. Keep spare remotes with working batteries and note who maintains the door. If the door is shared, faults are a matter for the condominium. If it is private, they are yours.

## Visual checks while you are away

Owners often never see their garage space between visits. During [vacant property inspections](/vacant-property-inspections-algarve/), garages and storage rooms can be included where keys are provided and the owner has asked for it. Visible damp, water on the floor and signs of interference can be recorded and photographed. Guardemar does not open safes or inventory contents.
