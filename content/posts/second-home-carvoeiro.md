Carvoeiro is one of the Western Algarve’s established second-home settings. Clifftop villas, landscaped gardens and apartments in managed developments can sit close together, yet they do not need the same inspection routine.

A useful local plan starts with the property, not the postcode. The question is not whether Carvoeiro is “looked after”, but what can change quietly while the owner is abroad.

## Villas, pools and the systems that keep running

Detached homes around Carvoeiro often include a pool, irrigation, external gates and several outdoor living areas. Those systems continue after the owner leaves. A pool company and a gardener each have a specialist remit. Neither is a substitute for a whole-property visual check of bathrooms, kitchens, shutters and accessible plumbing.

Irrigation can fail in either direction. Planting can dry out, or a damaged line can waste water against a wall, terrace or façade. Pool water level and appearance can change even when a maintenance visit is already in the diary. Outdoor furniture, gates, visible drainage and obvious exterior wear should form part of the same observation.

These checks are visual. They do not replace pool chemistry, plant husbandry, roof access or any technical diagnosis. If a change is found, the next step is to inform the owner and, where authorised, coordinate the appropriate qualified professional.

## Apartments and managed developments

An apartment or townhouse in a managed development has a different profile. Access may depend on a condominium, a reception or a shared gate. Mail, internal humidity and leaks that could affect a neighbouring unit become more important than garden irrigation.

The inspection still needs a written instruction: who may enter, how the alarm is left, where the internal water controls are, and how building-wide issues should be reported. Informal key circulation is particularly unhelpful in a shared building, because it becomes difficult to know who entered and when.

[Secure key holding](/key-holding-algarve/) and a record of authorised access give the owner a clearer account than a neighbour holding an unmarked set of keys.

## Coastal humidity and closed rooms

Carvoeiro’s coastal position brings salt-laden air and humidity as well as sunshine. Closed bathrooms, wardrobes and rooms with limited air movement can show the first signs of mustiness or surface mould during a long absence. A dated photograph is more useful than a message saying the house “looked fine”.

Humidity readings can add context, but trends, odours, surface changes and comparison with the previous visit usually matter more than one isolated number. The wider pattern is explained in the guide to [humidity and mould in Algarve holiday homes](/blog/humidity-mould-algarve-holiday-homes/).

A visual inspection can flag a change. Concealed pipework, waterproofing and wall cavities still belong with an appropriate specialist when there is reason for concern.

## Seasonal occupancy

Many Carvoeiro homes are used intensively during holiday periods and then stand quiet for several weeks or months. That pattern makes a documented baseline particularly useful. The first structured visit records the property’s normal condition. Later visits are then a comparison, not a first impression.

A new ceiling mark, a changed pool level, a dry irrigation zone or a displaced shutter has more meaning when the previous report is available. That is the practical value of a repeatable [inspection checklist](/inspection-checklist/).

Frequency should follow the home. A straightforward apartment may suit a monthly check. A villa with a pool, irrigation and several external access points may justify fortnightly or weekly visits. The guide to [inspection frequency for empty properties](/blog/how-often-inspect-empty-property-portugal/) explains the decision without pretending that one timetable fits every house.

## Build a local routine

Keep a current property profile: water shut-offs, electrical board, alarm instructions, pool and garden contacts, and who may authorise a contractor. Arrange [arrival preparation](/arrival-preparation-algarve/) before longer stays. After leaving, a departure check confirms that access points and agreed systems have been left as instructed.
