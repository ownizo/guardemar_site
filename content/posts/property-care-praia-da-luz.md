Praia da Luz has long attracted international owners, with apartments, townhouses and villas used for part of the year. That pattern makes continuity between visits particularly important.

## Common care priorities

Coastal humidity, shutters, terraces and ventilation are relevant across many property types. Villas often add pool condition, irrigation, external gates and garden observations. Apartments may depend more heavily on clear condominium access and communication.

## Separate oversight from maintenance

A gardener may identify a dry planting bed and a pool company may test water chemistry, but neither necessarily checks a bathroom ceiling or kitchen cupboard. Structured property care gives an owner a view across agreed areas of the home.

## Plan around seasonal vacancy

Before closing the property, document how water, electricity, hot water, appliances and ventilation should be left. During vacancy, use a consistent [home watch checklist](/inspection-checklist/). Before returning, arrange a visit early enough for any contractor follow-up.

## Use photographs as evidence

Images of rooms, access points and external areas help overseas owners distinguish “all okay” from a genuinely documented visit. A status should make any concern easy to prioritise.
