There is no single correct inspection frequency for every empty property. The right schedule reflects the home’s complexity, vacancy period, season and the owner’s tolerance for time passing before a problem is noticed.

## Monthly checks

A monthly visit can be a reasonable starting point for a straightforward apartment with few external systems, particularly where condominium management monitors common areas. It still leaves several weeks between observations, which owners should consider carefully.

## Twice-monthly checks

Fortnightly visits reduce the observation gap and suit many villas or townhouses with water, air conditioning, terraces, irrigation or a pool. They also create a more useful rhythm for ventilation and running water where those tasks are appropriate and agreed.

## Weekly checks

Weekly oversight may be preferable for larger villas, complex systems, known maintenance concerns or owners who want a higher service priority. Frequency does not make an inspection technical; it simply means visible changes are more likely to be reported sooner.

## Adjust through the year

Inspection needs can change around extended absences, major works or periods of severe weather. A property that receives monthly visits may need an additional post-storm or contractor follow-up visit.
