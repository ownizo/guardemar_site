Atlantic storms reach the Western Algarve every winter. Most pass without consequence. Occasionally, strong wind or heavy rain damages shutters, roofs, garden walls, pergolas or trees, or water finds its way inside. For an owner abroad, the first sign may be a neighbour's message or a gap in the camera feed.

A clear sequence makes the following days easier, both for repairs and for any insurance claim.

## 1. Wait until it is safe

Nobody should go up on roofs, under damaged structures or near fallen cables during or immediately after a storm. Where there is immediate danger, the emergency services come first. A visit should take place when conditions and access are safe.

## 2. Record the condition

Before anything is cleared or repaired, take dated photographs of:

- the exterior from several angles
- damaged shutters, windows, doors, gates and roof areas visible from the ground
- fallen trees, branches and displaced items
- terraces, drains and any standing water
- interior signs of water entry, such as ceilings, walls and floors near openings

Keep a short note of what was found and when. Read [what to check after a storm in the Algarve](/blog/what-check-after-storm-algarve/).

## 3. Limit further damage

Reasonable temporary measures, such as securing a loose shutter, covering a broken window or moving items away from water, are usually expected. Keep receipts for any emergency work.

## 4. Notify your insurer

Check your policy for the claim procedure and time limits. Storm cover often depends on how the policy defines a storm. Some policies refer to wind speeds or to official weather records. Make a note of the date and keep links to weather warnings issued for the area.

## 5. Get quotes and keep the evidence

Ask builders, roofers or glaziers for written quotes that describe the damage. Do not dispose of damaged items until you know whether the insurer wants to see them. If an assessor (perito) is sent, someone will need to provide access.

## 6. Follow up

Water that entered during a storm can lead to damp and mould weeks later. A follow-up visit can record whether affected areas are drying. Read [humidity and mould in Algarve holiday homes](/blog/humidity-mould-algarve-holiday-homes/).
