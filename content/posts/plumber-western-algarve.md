Almost every Algarve second home will need a plumber at some point. A dripping mixer tap, a cistern that keeps running, a water heater that has stopped working, or a damp patch under the kitchen sink. None of these is unusual. What makes them harder is distance: the owner is in London, Amsterdam or Toronto, and the property is closed.

Finding someone competent is only part of the problem. The plumber also needs keys, information and a clear decision about what they are allowed to do.

## Start before there is a problem

The best time to find a plumber is when nothing is leaking. Ask neighbours, your condominium administrator, the agent who sold you the property, or other providers who already work at the house. Pool and garden teams often know which local trades turn up when they say they will.

Keep at least two names. Good plumbers in the Western Algarve are busy, particularly before Easter and in early summer, when many owners return at once.

## What to record for each plumber

A short entry in your property file saves time later:

- name, company and mobile number
- languages spoken, and whether they send written quotes by email or WhatsApp
- whether they issue invoices with your tax number (NIF)
- work they have already done at the property
- whether they are happy to work with a keyholder present

## Brief them with facts, not guesses

When something goes wrong, the plumber needs more than "there is a leak". Useful information includes where the water is visible, when it was first noticed, whether the supply has been turned off, and photographs of the area. A dated [photo report](/blog/what-photo-report-should-contain/) from a recent visit is often the quickest way to show what changed.

The plumber diagnoses and repairs. A property-care contact should not guess at causes or present a visual observation as a technical finding.

## Agree spending limits in advance

Many delays come from authority, not availability. Decide in writing:

- who can call the plumber on your behalf
- the amount that can be approved without asking you first
- whether the water can be turned off at the main stopcock where it is safe to do so
- how quotes and invoices reach you
