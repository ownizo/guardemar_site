Air-conditioning that has been switched off for six months does not always switch back on smoothly. Filters collect dust, drains block, outdoor units corrode in the salt air and remote controls lose their batteries. Owners often find out on the first hot day, when every technician in the region is already busy.

## Why servicing matters in a second home

In a home that is lived in all year, problems with air-conditioning appear gradually. In a second home, they all appear at once, usually on arrival. A service before the season gives time to order parts and repair faults before anyone travels.

Air-conditioning units also help with humidity when used in dehumidifying mode. If you rely on that during absences, the system needs to be working properly. See [humidity and mould in Algarve holiday homes](/blog/humidity-mould-algarve-holiday-homes/).

## What a service usually covers

Ask your technician what is included. A typical service may cover:

- cleaning or replacing filters
- cleaning indoor units and checking the drainage
- checking outdoor units for corrosion, debris and secure mounting
- checking operation in cooling, heating and dehumidifying modes
- reporting anything that needs repair

Work on refrigerant circuits is regulated and must be carried out by suitably qualified technicians.

## When to book

In the Western Algarve, demand rises sharply from May. Booking in March or April is usually easier. For homes used at Christmas or in winter, a check of the heating mode in October or November makes sense.

## Outdoor units in coastal homes

Outdoor units on terraces and roofs near the sea are exposed to salt, sand and wind. Ask the technician to look at fixings, panels and connections. See [salt air, shutters and outdoor metalwork](/blog/salt-air-shutters-outdoor-metalwork-algarve/).
