Most owners install an alarm for peace of mind. Fewer think carefully about what happens next. When the alarm triggers at three in the morning, who is told, who goes to the property, and who has the key?

## Monitored and unmonitored alarms

An **unmonitored** alarm sounds a siren and, perhaps, sends a notification to the owner's phone. If the owner is abroad, the response depends on whether they can reach someone locally.

A **monitored** alarm is connected to a monitoring centre operated by a private security company. When the alarm triggers, the centre follows an agreed procedure. Depending on the contract, that may include trying to verify the alarm, calling the owner and keyholders, and alerting the police where appropriate.

In Portugal, private security companies, including those that provide alarm monitoring, operate under licensing and supervision by the PSP. Ask your provider about its licence and the response it actually provides.

## Questions to ask your alarm company

- What happens when an alarm is triggered, step by step?
- Does the centre verify alarms, for example with cameras or sensors?
- Does the service include a patrol or guard response, or only phone calls?
- Who are the keyholders on the contact list, and in what order are they called?
- What happens if nobody on the list answers?
- Is there battery backup, and is a power cut reported?
- Who resets the system after an event?

## The keyholder gap

Many alarm contracts assume there is someone nearby who can attend, open the door and reset the system. For overseas owners, that is often the weakest link: a neighbour who is away, a former cleaner whose number changed, or nobody at all.

A professional keyholder can be named on the contact list, with clear written authority. Guardemar does not monitor alarms and is not a security company. ## False alarms

Insects, pets, curtains moving in a draught, power fluctuations and storms all cause false alarms. Repeated false alarms are a nuisance and can undermine response. If your alarm triggers often, ask the installer to review sensor positions and settings.
