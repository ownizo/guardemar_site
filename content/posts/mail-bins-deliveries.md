An empty house still receives things. Post arrives. Bins are not put out. A parcel is left at the door. None of this is dramatic on day two. After a month it is a pile that tells the lane the property is vacant, and a smell that tells the next person who opens the kitchen.

These are unglamorous checks. They belong on the same visit as leaks and shutters.

## Mail is both information and a signal

A stuffed letterbox is one of the simplest vacancy signs. It is also how an official-looking envelope sits unread. A keyholder can collect mail, photograph anything that looks as if it needs the owner’s attention, and store the rest as instructed.

They should not open correspondence as a matter of routine unless you have given that authority. The job is to prevent accumulation and to flag what you asked them to flag.

Mailbox keys belong in the same coded inventory as the door key. See [key holding](/blog/key-holding-portugal-overseas-owners/).

## Bins and waste need an instruction

If you left in a hurry, perishable waste may still be in the kitchen. A departure check should catch that. During vacancy, decide whether a cleaner or keyholder puts bins out according to the local collection, or whether the house simply stays closed and empty of rubbish.

Apartment bin stores and shared chutes have their own rules. A condominium may object to overflow that started in your kitchen. The property file should say where waste goes and who is allowed to deal with it.

Do not leave standing water in trays, buckets or plant saucers as a side effect of “we will sort the garden later”. That is how you invite pests. See [pest signs](/blog/pest-signs-empty-algarve-home/).

## Stop deliveries you cannot receive

Online orders to an unoccupied address are an invitation to leave a box on a step. Redirect them, pause them, or authorise a person to accept them. If a delivery must happen — a spare part, a pool chemical the technician ordered — [contractor access](/contractor-access-algarve/) or a documented handover is safer than a card in the door.

Neighbours should not become an informal depot unless they have agreed, and even then the arrangement should be written down. [Neighbour, house-sitter or professional keyholder](/blog/neighbour-housesitter-or-professional-keyholder/) is the wider access conversation.

## Put it on the checklist

Mail, visible waste and obvious parcels take little time and tell you a lot about whether the house still looks occupied. They sit naturally in the [inspection checklist](/inspection-checklist/) beside doors and water points.

If this is part of the reason you want a local visit routine, compare [plans](/plans/) or [request a property assessment](/contact/).
